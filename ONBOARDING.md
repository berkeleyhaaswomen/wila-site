# Welcome to the WILA Content Studio

You've been invited to help maintain **wila.haasalumni.org**. This guide gets
you from zero to publishing your first update in under 10 minutes.

You don't need to know code. Everything happens in a friendly web editor.

---

## 1. Accept your invitation

Check your inbox for an email from **Sanity** with the subject
"You've been invited to WILA Content".

Click **Accept invitation**. You'll be asked to sign in — pick whichever is
easiest:

- **Google** (recommended) — one click if your email is Google-backed
- **GitHub**
- **Email** — Sanity sends a magic link, no password to remember

That's it — you now have an account.

---

## 2. Open the Studio

Go to: **[https://wila.haasalumni.org/studio](https://wila.haasalumni.org/studio)**

You'll land in the Content Studio. In the sidebar on the left you'll see:

- **Site settings** — hero copy, About section, contact info
- **Events** — everything on the Events section of the site
- **Alumna spotlights** — the featured alumna and past features
- **Board members** — the leadership grid

If you can see this sidebar, you're in. 🎉

---

## 3. Your role, at a glance

The Co-Presidents assigned you one of these roles:

| Your role | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Editor** (most people) | Create, edit, and publish any content. Cannot manage other users. |
| **Administrator** | Everything above, plus invite/remove users and change roles. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only — you can review drafts but not edit. |

Not sure which one you have? Ask a Co-President, or just try — the Studio will
tell you if an action isn't allowed.

---

## 4. Make your first edit (practice run)

Let's post a fake event, then delete it. This gives you the feel of the tool
without touching real content.

1. Click **Events** in the sidebar
2. Click the **+ Create** button at the top
3. Fill in:
   - **Event title:** `Test event — please ignore`
   - **Slug:** click the auto-generate icon
   - **Starts at:** any future date
   - **Location:** `Test location`
   - **Format:** pick any
   - **Blurb:** `This is a test.`
4. Click **Publish** in the top right
5. Wait ~2 minutes, then check the site — your test event will appear under
   "Upcoming"
6. Come back to the Studio, open the test event, click the **⋯** menu →
   **Delete** to clean up

You now know how to publish anything.

## 5. Common tasks

**Post a real event**
Events → **+ Create** → fill in the fields → **Publish**.
Events auto-sort into "Upcoming" and "Past" based on their start date, so you
never have to move them by hand.

**Update someone's board bio**
Board members → click their name → update role, LinkedIn, or photo → **Publish**.
The **Display order** field controls the sort — Co-Presidents should be 1-3,
other board members can be 10+.

**Rotate the alumnae spotlight**
Alumna spotlights → **+ Create** → add name, quote, bio, LinkedIn, photo →
set **Featured from** to today → **Publish**.
The homepage automatically shows the spotlight with the most recent
"Featured from" date. Old spotlights stay in the Studio for reference.

**Fix a typo in the hero copy**
Site settings (pinned at the top) → edit the field → **Publish**.

## 6. Good habits

- **Preview before publishing.** Sanity shows an unpublished draft in the
  editor with a yellow strip at the top. Use the **Split pane** button
  (top-right of the editor) to see the field values before hitting Publish.
- **Photos: 400×400 or larger.** Square is best for board avatars.
- **Blurbs stay short.** Aim for 1-3 sentences. The design falls apart with
  wall-of-text descriptions.
- **When in doubt, save as draft.** Every field has an implicit draft — you
  can close the tab and come back without losing work. Nothing goes live
  until you hit **Publish**.

## 7. When something goes wrong

**"I can't log in."**
Try the "Log in with email" option — Sanity sends a fresh magic link each
time, so there's no password to reset. If the invitation email link expired,
ask a Co-President to re-invite you.

**"My change isn't showing on the site."**
The site rebuilds within a few minutes of Publish, but it's not instant.
Give it 5 minutes and hard-refresh the page (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R). If it's
still missing after 15 minutes, ping the Co-Presidents.

**"I deleted something by accident."**
Sanity keeps a full revision history. Open the document → click the clock
icon in the top-right → find the version you want → **Restore**.

**"The Studio looks broken."**
Try a hard refresh (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R) or an incognito window. If it
persists, screenshot the error and send it to the Co-Presidents.
